What's around Amani Stays

Naivasha rewards slow mornings and short drives. Here's what most guests build a day around.

Boat rides on the lake

Hippos, fish eagles and a still shoreline, best booked for early morning or golden hour.

Hell's Gate & wildlife walks

Cycle or walk among zebra and giraffe, a short transfer from the hotel, arranged through our team.

Sunset on the escarpment

A short drive up for views back over the lake, a favourite for the Romantic Getaway package.
